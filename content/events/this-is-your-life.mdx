Join us on Sunday 15th November for a special ‘This Is Your Life’ event.

Joni will be interviewing a mystery guest from our church, who has no idea what's coming! We'll take a look back over their life, hearing stories, sharing memories and discovering some of the experiences and people who have helped shape their journey.

It'll be a relaxed and enjoyable time together, with plenty of interesting stories along the way and perhaps a few surprises too!

We'd love you to join us for this special Sunday.
