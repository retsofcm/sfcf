Join us for an inspiring evening as we explore God’s heart for the nations and consider how we can play a part in sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with those who have never heard it.

There is a unique opportunity to step into the world of two very different villages in Papua New Guinea, guided by missionaries who have worked there. In one village, you’ll discover the life-changing impact of people coming to faith in Jesus and receiving the Bible in their own language. In the other, you’ll gain a glimpse of life in a community that has yet to hear the good news. These interactive experiences will help us understand why reaching unreached people groups matters so much.

We’ll also hear a challenge from Southeast Asia, where 100 new workers are needed to help bring the gospel to those who have yet to hear it. You’ll meet students training for mission at North Cotes College in Lincolnshire and hear something of their journeys.

The Bible focus of the evening is Jesus’ compelling invitation to: “Follow me.”

Come along to be encouraged, challenged and inspired. Discover more about God’s heart for the nations and how you could be part of His mission to reach those who have never heard the name of Jesus.
