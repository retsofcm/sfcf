Join us Sunday 22nd November as we welcome New Tribes Mission to lead our service.

New Tribes Mission (known as Ethnos360 in many parts of the world) is a missionary organisation committed to sharing the good news of Jesus with people groups around the world who have little or no access to the Gospel.

We'll hear more about their work, their passion for reaching unreached communities and the difference that sharing God's love and message can make. It will be a great opportunity to learn more about global mission and how we can play a part in supporting the work of sharing the Gospel.

We'd love you to join us!
