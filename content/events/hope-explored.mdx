We believe church is more than just a Sunday meeting. It’s a community where you can explore the big questions of life. Join us on May 31st as we look at the life-changing news of Jesus and why his message still brings hope today. Whether you’re curious about faith or haven’t stepped into a church in years, you are welcome exactly as you are.

The conversation doesn't end with the service. Please stay afterward for tea, coffee, and refreshments. We’d love to get to know you!
