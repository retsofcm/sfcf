Join us from 16th to 22nd June, 6:45 to 8 PM each evening for Real Lives -
a week-long series of inspiring interviews with people whose lives have
been changed by faith. Hosted by Roger Carswell, these honest and
uplifting stories offer hope, encouragement, and a chance to explore what
faith means in everyday life.

There’s also a children’s program running during the talks, so the whole
family can enjoy the event.
