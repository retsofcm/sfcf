A big thank you to everyone who joined us for our Remembrance Sunday service this year.

It was a privilege to come together to remember and give thanks for those who made the ultimate sacrifice in service to our country, protecting the freedoms we enjoy today.

"There is no greater love than to lay down one’s life for one’s friends."- John 15:13

We were also grateful to hear from Ryan, who served on the frontlines with the Royal Marines in Afghanistan. Ryan shared some of his personal experiences, as well as how coming to know Jesus transformed his life.

If Ryan’s story resonated with you, or you’d like to find out more about his journey of faith and what it means to know Jesus, we’d love to hear from you. We also host a 12-week Just Looking course for anyone curious about exploring Christianity in a relaxed, no-pressure setting. You are welcome to ask questions or just listen.
