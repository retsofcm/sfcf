      Stenson Fields Christian Fellowship began through a children’s ministry at
      the local school, which was first established in 19??. As children and
      their families came to trust in Christ, a small group of local believers
      recognised the need for a church rooted in the heart of Stenson Fields.
      They began meeting in homes around 19??, praying and planning until, in
      19??, God opened the door for a piece of land to be purchased on Pilgrims
      Way. Through His clear provision, the finances were raised to buy the land
      and build the church building that still serves as our base today.


      From those small beginnings, God has continued to grow the work. Today,
      Stenson Fields Christian Fellowship is involved in a range of ministries,
      from toddler groups and coffee mornings to family events and the
      children’s ministry Explorers, which sparked it all. At the heart of
      everything we do is a desire to share the good news of Jesus with our
      community.


      A key moment in our year is always Real Lives week, a time when families
      gather to hear powerful, personal stories of faith. It’s a particular joy
      to now welcome children whose parents once came to those same kids’ clubs,
      as they return each Friday night for songs, games, Bible stories, and the
      hope that continues to shape our church: that more lives might be reached
      and transformed by Christ.
