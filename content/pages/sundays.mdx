  - heading: Kids on Sundays
    body: >
      We love welcoming children of all ages and want them to feel at home in
      church. After the first part of the morning service, just before the
      sermon begins, children are invited to head out to their own groups for
      age-appropriate activities, stories, games, and Bible teaching.


      * **Crèche** - For babies and toddlers up to nursery age. A safe and
      caring space for our youngest children to play and be looked after.

      * **Sunday Club** - For children from Reception to Year 5. Each week
      includes fun, interactive Bible stories, crafts, and time to pray and
      learn together.

      * **Years 6–8 Group** - A space for older primary and younger
      secondary-aged children to explore faith, ask questions, and engage with
      the Bible in a way that connects with their stage of life.


      All of our children’s leaders are friendly, experienced, and DBS-checked.
