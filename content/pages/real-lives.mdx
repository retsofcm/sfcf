    content: >
      Kai Höss is the grandson of Rudolf Höss, the commandant of Auschwitz.
      Raised in a non-Christian home, Kai pursued a career in the hospitality
      industry, enjoying material success. However, a near-death experience led
      him to read the Bible, and he became a Christian in 1989. Today, he serves
      as the pastor of the Bible Church of Stuttgart, sharing a message of
      grace, forgiveness, and reconciliation. Kai uses his family's history to
      speak out against antisemitism and to promote understanding between
      Christians and Jews.
